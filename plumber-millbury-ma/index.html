Winter readiness

Cold snaps are the common cause of burst pipes in Central Massachusetts. Keep heat on, open cabinets on exterior walls and let a tap drip during hard freezes. If you will be away, ask a neighbor to check the house. A frozen pipe that has not yet split can be thawed safely by a plumber.

Basements near the river

Millbury lies along the Blackstone River, and homes near the river or the old canal corridor can see groundwater rise after heavy rain and spring melt. A basement that stays damp in wet seasons usually has a drainage cause. Start with gutters, downspouts and grading, then look at the sump pit. If your pump runs constantly or fails, our sump pump repair page explains the next steps.

A pump alone does not fix every wet basement. We trace the source before recommending drainage work, and we tell you honestly when the water comes from a supply line instead.

Water heaters and boilers in older homes

Millbury has many older houses with boilers and aging tanks. A hot water tank that has run for a long time can fail without warning, and a boiler that quits in January puts pipes at risk. See water heater replacement and boiler repair for what to do first.

Winter heating failures in Millbury

When a boiler or furnace quits on a cold night, pipes in exterior walls and crawlspaces start the clock on freezing. Open cabinet doors, keep interior doors open to share heat and let a tap drip. If the house falls below a safe temperature for long, consider draining the lines. Call the 24/7 line and see our boiler repair page.

Sewer lines under mature trees

Streets in Millbury have mature trees, and roots find cracks in old drain lines. If you have slow drains that clear and return, ask for a camera look. The recording shows roots, sags and offsets, and our sewer inspection page explains what you will see.
