No heat: safe checks before you call

Gas smell is an emergency. Leave the building and call from outside. Do not light anything or flip switches. If there is no gas smell, check that the thermostat is calling for heat, the boiler switch is on, and the pressure gauge reads in the normal range printed on the unit.

  • Check thermostat batteries and setting.
  • Confirm the emergency switch and breaker are on.
  • Look at the pressure gauge. Very low or very high pressure needs service.
  • Do not refill or bleed repeatedly without knowing why pressure drops.
  • Keep pipes from freezing by opening cabinet doors and dripping a tap if the house is cold.

Winter heating in Grafton

Grafton winters are cold enough that a heat failure can freeze pipes within hours in an uninsulated wall. Older houses near the historic districts of Fisherville and Farnumsville often have baseboard or radiator systems that have run for decades. Annual service keeps a boiler dependable. Do it in the fall, not in January.

If the house will be cold for more than a few hours, drain risk is real. Ask us about protecting pipes while the boiler is repaired. Frozen pipes are covered on our burst pipe repair page.

Radiators, baseboards and cold rooms

A boiler that runs while some rooms stay cold usually has air trapped in the loop, a stuck zone valve or a failing circulator. Bleeding air is easy on some systems and tricky on others, because opening a bleeder on a pressurized loop without understanding the fill valve can drop pressure fast. We purge the loop properly, check the expansion tank and tell you how to spot the problem early next time.

Carbon monoxide and boiler safety

Fuel-burning boilers produce carbon monoxide when something goes wrong. Keep working detectors on every level and near sleeping areas, and treat an alarm as an emergency. Leave the house, call 911 and wait for responders.
