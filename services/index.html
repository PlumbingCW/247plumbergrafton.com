What to do first in any plumbing emergency

  • Close the main water shutoff by turning it clockwise.
  • If you smell gas, leave and call from outside. Do not touch switches.
  • Stay away from wet outlets, the panel and standing water near electrics.
  • Photograph damage before you clean up, then call the 24/7 line.
