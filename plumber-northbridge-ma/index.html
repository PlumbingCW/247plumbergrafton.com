Mill-era housing and older plumbing

Whitinsville and Rockdale grew up around mills, and housing from that era still stands. Older homes can have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains, both of which wear from the inside. If your pipes have a history of leaks, repiping may end the cycle. We show you where the problems are before we recommend it.

Newer subdivisions have PEX and PVC, which behave differently. A leak in plastic pipe is usually a fitting. We find it, fix it and tell you what to watch.

Basements and groundwater

After spring melt and big storms, basements near the rivers take on water. A sump pump is your first defense. If it fails, call. The basement waterproofing page explains what we check beyond the pump.

Burst pipes and winter in Northbridge

A pipe that freezes in an exterior wall in Rockdale or Linwood looks the same as one in Whitinsville. Water stops at a tap, and then a crack leaks when the ice melts. Close the main at the first sign, open a low tap and call. A frozen pipe caught early can be thawed before it splits.

Sewer, septic and water in Northbridge

Parts of Northbridge are on town services and others are on septic or private wells. Knowing which you have changes what an emergency looks like. A well pump failure leaves you without water. A septic backup shows up as slow drains and wet ground. Tell the dispatcher which system you have when you call, and we will arrive prepared.

If you are not sure, check your bills and property records, or ask the town office. Our sewer line cleaning page explains what happens when a house line blocks.

Questions the dispatcher will ask

Expect to be asked your address, what is happening, whether the main is closed and whether anyone is in danger. If you smell gas, we send you outside before we ask anything else. The answers let us dispatch the right plumber with the right parts, and they help us advise you while we are on the way.
