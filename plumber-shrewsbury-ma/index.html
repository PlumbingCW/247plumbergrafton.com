Shrewsbury homes, busy corridors and what breaks

Shrewsbury is a large suburb stretched along Route 9 and Route 20 with I-290 on its west side. It has dense neighborhoods, condos and a busy commercial corridor, so the same weekend can bring a burst pipe in a single-family house and a drain backup in a ground-floor retail unit. We handle both.

Many homes are on town water and sewer, which changes who owns what. The pipe inside your property line is yours to repair. Pipes in the street belong to the town. If you are unsure where the line ends, call and we will tell you before any digging begins.

Emergency calls from Shrewsbury

  • Burst or frozen pipe: close the main and open a low tap.
  • Sewage in a floor drain or tub: stop all water use.
  • Water heater leaking or no hot water.
  • Boiler off in winter.
  • Basement water after a storm.

Pick the match, then call. Our pages on sewer line cleaning and boiler repair explain what happens next.

Lake Quinsigamond and low ground

Shrewsbury shares Lake Quinsigamond with Worcester. Homes near the lake shore and other low ground can see wet basements after storms, so a working sump pump matters. If yours runs nonstop or not at all, see sump pump repair.

Condos and multi-family buildings add another wrinkle. A leak can start in one unit and show up in another, and shared stacks serve several units. We trace the source and describe it in a way you can hand to an association or landlord.

Commercial and multi-unit plumbing

Restaurants, offices and retail units along the commercial strips need after-hours help that does not wait for Monday. See commercial plumbing for what we handle and what needs a specialist.

Older plumbing in a large town

Shrewsbury has housing from many decades, so one street can hold a house with original galvanized pipe next to a house piped in plastic. Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, narrows the pipe and eventually leaks. If your water runs brown after sitting overnight or pressure has slowly fallen, the pipe may be closing up. Our repiping page explains how we map the system and replace only what needs it.

Water heaters age out too. A tank with rust at the base or a puddle underneath is at the end of its life, and our emergency replacement page explains the fast route.

Winter calls from Shrewsbury

Cold weather brings frozen pipes along exterior walls, boilers that quit and sump pumps that freeze. Shrewsbury homes along open ridges and lake shores take the wind. Keep cabinet doors open on outside walls, let a tap trickle in a hard freeze and call before a frozen pipe splits. A thaw by a plumber is cheaper than a flood.
