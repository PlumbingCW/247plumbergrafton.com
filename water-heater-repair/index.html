No hot water or a leaking tank: what to do first

Start with what you can see. Water pooled around the base and dripping from the tank body means the tank has failed internally, and no repair fixes that. Water dripping from a pipe fitting, the relief valve, or the drain valve is a smaller failure, and a plumber can often fix it the same visit.

  • Leaking from the bottom: shut off the cold supply and power, then call us.
  • Dripping from the relief valve pipe: do not cap it, because the valve is there to protect the tank.
  • No hot water, no leak: check the breaker or the pilot light, then call.
  • Rumbling or popping noises: sediment is cooking at the bottom of the tank, and that usually calls for a flush before it damages the tank.

Callers often say the water is hot but runs out in a few minutes. That points to a broken dip tube, a failed lower heating element on an electric unit, or a tank full of sediment. All three are repairable when the tank itself is sound.

How we diagnose a water heater that stopped working

Gas units are tested for the pilot, the thermocouple, the gas valve and the venting. A pilot that will not stay lit is usually a worn thermocouple. A burner that lights then shuts off often has a draft or venting problem. Electric units are tested for power at the element, the thermostat and the high-limit reset. We use a meter instead of guessing, because a bad element and a bad thermostat look the same from the shower.

Tankless units have their own failure modes. Error codes, a cold-water sandwich that comes and goes, and reduced flow usually point to scale on the heat exchanger or a clogged inlet screen. If your tankless unit is due for descaling, our tankless water heater service covers flush and descale as its own visit.

When repair stops making sense

A tank that is old, leaking from the shell, and wearing a patchwork of earlier repairs is a poor place to put more money. We say so. A water heater past its normal service life with a new failure is usually cheaper to replace than to repair twice, and a failed tank can release the full contents onto your floor with little warning.

Age is on the label. The serial number on the data plate encodes the manufacture date, and the manufacturer posts how to read it. If you cannot find it, send us the model number and we will help. We also look at the venting, the shutoff valves and the expansion tank, because a repair done next to a corroded valve invites the next call.

Grafton water and your tank

Grafton Water District and South Grafton Water District both draw groundwater from wells. The water districts publish annual quality reports, and homes on private wells have no such report at all. We do not quote a hardness number for the town because the figure depends on which system and which well supplies your street. Test your tap, or ask your water district for its annual report, before choosing between a standard tank and a tankless unit.

Hard water speeds up sediment in the tank and scale in a tankless exchanger, which is why we ask about it on every visit. A flush once a year is cheap compared with replacing a unit early. Homes in North Grafton and South Grafton near Route 122 are good examples, since neighbors can sit on different supplies.

If a repair visit shows corroded shutoff valves or a missing expansion tank, we point that out before leaving. Fixing them during a water heater installation later is simpler than finding them during an emergency.

Questions about water heater repair in Grafton

Do you handle water heater emergencies at night or on weekends in Grafton?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls any hour. For an active leak, shut off the cold supply to the tank first and then call.

Why is my water heater leaking from the top?

Top leaks usually come from a loose fitting, a bad relief valve, or condensation. A plumber checks each. A leak from the bottom of the shell is different and usually means the tank has failed.

Can you repair a tankless water heater?

Yes. Most tankless service calls involve descaling, inlet screens, vent checks or error codes. Replacement is for failed heat exchangers or boards that cost too much to repair.

How do I know if I should repair or replace?

Age, leak location, and how many repairs the unit has already had decide it. We give you both numbers when the answer is close.

Is a pilot light that keeps going out dangerous?

It can be. A gas unit that cannot hold a pilot may have a venting or gas supply problem. If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 before calling us.
