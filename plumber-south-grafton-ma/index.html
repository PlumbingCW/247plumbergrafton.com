Where South Grafton is

South Grafton is the southern village of the Town of Grafton and has its own ZIP code, 01560. The South Grafton Water District has its state-listed address at 8A Main Street, and one of its wells is off Providence Road, which is Route 122. The mill villages of Fisherville and Farnumsville are nearby in southwest Grafton, and we cover them on their own pages.

We did not find a published street-by-street boundary for South Grafton, so we use Route 122, Main Street and Ferry Street as anchors.

Your own water district

South Grafton has its own public water system, the South Grafton Water District, separate from the Grafton Water District that serves other parts of town. The state source water report lists three wells: one off Providence Road and two off Ferry Street. All three add potassium hydroxide for corrosion control.

That matters when you plan plumbing work. If you are replacing a water heater, a service line or a main shutoff, the water district may have its own rules for the meter and the connection at the street. Ask the district before you dig or cut. Water service line repair is the kind of job where the answer changes the plan.

Sewer or septic: confirm, do not assume

The state report notes that about 30 percent of the land in the district's protection areas is residential, and that most but not all of those areas have public sewers. That suggests some homes in the area use septic systems. A slow drain on a septic system can point to the tank or leach field and not to the pipe in the house.

If you are not sure, find out before you call for drain cleaning or a sewer camera inspection. A plumber can work faster when you say up front whether the house is on sewer or septic. If the problem is the septic system itself, a plumber may refer you to a septic specialist.

Questions about plumbing in South Grafton

What is the South Grafton Water District?

It is a public water system in Grafton, separate from the Grafton Water District. Its state-listed address is 8A Main Street and its wells are off Providence Road and Ferry Street.

How do I know which water district I am in?

Check your water bill. It names the system. If you have no bill because you use a private well, the district does not serve you.

Is South Grafton on town sewer?

Parts of it are. A state report says most but not all residential areas near the district's wells have public sewers. Confirm your address with the Town of Grafton.

Who owns the pipe between the street and my house?

It depends where the district's responsibility ends and yours begins. Ask the water district before any excavation.
