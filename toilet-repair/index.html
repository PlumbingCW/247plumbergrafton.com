Toilet overflowing: stop the water first

Do not flush again. Close the shutoff valve at the wall, usually a small oval handle behind the toilet. If it will not turn, lift the float or lift the lid and press the flapper to stop the tank refilling. Mop standing water and open windows if sewage is involved.

  • If the water is clear tank water, it is an inside-the-house leak, and a towel and the shutoff stop the damage.
  • If the water is dirty and other drains are slow, the main line may be blocked. See sewer line cleaning.
  • If it is only this toilet, a plunger or an auger usually clears it.
  • Call us if water has reached the floor below.

A toilet that rocks or a floor that feels soft near it is a sign to act. Water at the base can rot the subfloor over time. If the flange is damaged, we repair it before setting the toilet. Related fixtures such as sinks are covered on our faucet repair page.

Installing a new toilet

Replacing a toilet is more than swapping a fixture. We check the flange height, the supply valve, the floor condition and the rough-in distance so the new unit fits. Water-saving models are common, and we help you pick a size and height that suits your household.

If the old toilet wobbled for years, we check whether water has damaged the floor. Better to find it now than behind a wall later.

Toilets in Grafton homes

Older Grafton homes, including those near the Fisherville and Farnumsville historic districts, can have cast iron closet bends and old flanges. Newer homes use PVC. Homes on septic should avoid flushable wipes and harsh bowl cleaners, which can upset the tank. Grafton Water District and South Grafton Water District supply well water, and mineral buildup in the tank is common in many areas, so we test your fill valve and flapper for wear as part of any visit.

Clogs: plunger, auger and when to stop

A flange plunger with a good seal clears most toilet clogs. A toilet auger reaches farther and avoids scratching the bowl. If both fail, or if the water backs up into the tub or a floor drain, the problem is past the toilet. Stop, because repeated flushing sends more water into a blocked line. Call, and see our sewer line cleaning page to understand what comes next.

Children and toys are a frequent cause. If something solid went down, tell us. A closet auger or pulling the toilet may be the right way to recover it without damage.

Questions about toilet repair and installation in Grafton

Do you handle toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Grafton?

Yes. Close the shutoff valve and call the 24/7 line.

Why does my toilet run on its own?

A worn flapper or fill valve is the usual cause. Parts are inexpensive and fast to replace.

Is a leaking toilet base serious?

It can be. Water at the base can damage the floor. Reset with a new seal promptly.

Can you install a comfort-height toilet?

Yes. We also check the flange and supply before we set it.

What should I do if my toilet backs up into the tub?

Stop using water. The main line may be blocked. Call us.
