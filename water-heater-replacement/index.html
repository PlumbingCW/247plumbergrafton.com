A failed tank at night: the first fifteen minutes

Water heaters fail in two ways. Most slowly weep from a fitting or a corroded valve, which gives you time. Some fail at the tank seam and release the contents quickly. If you hear running water you cannot trace, touch the floor around the heater before you touch the heater.

  • Turn off the water heater at the breaker or turn the gas control to off.
  • Close the cold-water supply valve on the pipe above the tank.
  • Open a hot water tap upstairs to relieve vacuum.
  • Move belongings off the floor and call the 24/7 line.

If you cannot get to the valve safely, shut off the main water to the house. Find it today and tag it, because looking for it during a leak wastes the minutes that matter.

What we replace and what we recommend

We start by measuring what you have: tank size, fuel type, vent type, and the space it fits in. A like-for-like swap is the fastest path back to hot water. If your household has changed, say a growing family or a home office with a bathroom, we talk about capacity before we talk about brand.

Why replacement day is also inspection day

Replacing a water heater is a good time to catch problems that cause the next call. We check the relief valve and discharge pipe, the shutoff valves, the expansion tank, the venting, the gas connection, and the water pressure. Pressure that is too high shortens the life of every fixture and appliance in the home.

In Massachusetts, plumbing work is done by licensed plumbers and falls under the state plumbing code, 248 CMR 10.00. You can verify any plumber through the state Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters license lookup. Planning a replacement before the tank fails

Replacing on your schedule is cheaper and calmer. A tank that has started leaking from a fitting, makes sustained popping noises, or is well beyond a typical service life is a good candidate. Book a planned installation and you can pick the unit instead of taking what a supply house has on the shelf.

Homes in Grafton, from older houses in South Grafton to newer builds in North Grafton, often have basement water heaters with floor drains nearby. If yours sits on a finished floor, a drain pan with a drain line can save a lot of damage if the tank ever fails.

How long does a replacement take?

A like-for-like swap often takes a few hours. A change in fuel type or venting takes longer. We give you the expected time when we quote.

Should I switch to tankless?

It depends on your usage, gas line, venting and budget. We lay out both paths. A tank is simpler. Tankless saves space and gives continuous hot water.
