Last updated November 13, 2026. This page explains what information Grafton 24/7 Plumbing collects through this website and by phone.

What we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or comment sections, so it collects very little. Our host and analytics tools may record standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. If you call or email us, we collect the details you share so we can help you.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded or monitored for quality and training. See the terms of use for the call recording notice. A recording may include your name, address and a description of your plumbing problem.

How we use information

  • To dispatch a plumber and provide service.
  • To follow up on a job.
  • To improve this website and our service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share details with the plumbers who serve your call and with service providers who help run the website and phone line, and when the law requires it.

Cookies and third parties

Pages embed Google Maps and load fonts from Google. Those services may set their own cookies and are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, email contact@247plumbergrafton.com. We will respond as the law requires.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

Where we dispatch from

Our dispatch line covers Grafton, North Grafton, South Grafton and the nearby towns of Westborough, Shrewsbury, Millbury, Northbridge and Upton.
