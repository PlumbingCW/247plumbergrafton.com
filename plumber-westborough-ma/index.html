Plumbing emergencies we answer in Westborough

A plumbing emergency is any problem that damages the house or makes it unsafe to stay in while you wait. In Westborough that usually means a pipe that has split, a toilet or floor drain that is pushing sewage back, a water heater leaking onto the floor, or a basement taking on water. Each has a first step you can take in the minutes before we arrive.

What to do before the plumber arrives

The next ten minutes decide how much damage the house takes. Use the card below, and photograph anything wet before you clean it up.

Before we arrive: four things to do

Water, power and gas are the three hazards. Handle them in this order.

1.Stop the water.Close the main shutoff, usually where the line enters the house, by turning it clockwise.
2.Make power safe.If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out and switch off power only if the breaker is dry and reachable.
3.Check for gas.If you smell gas, leave and call from outside. Do not flip switches.
4.Document it.Photograph damage and keep receipts for your insurer.

Lake Chauncy, wet basements and older drains

Homes close to Lake Chauncy and other low ground in town can see higher groundwater after storms and spring melt. That puts a sump pump to work, and a pump that fails during a storm turns a damp basement into a flooded one. Test yours by pouring a bucket of water into the pit before the wet season.

Older homes may also have clay or cast iron drain lines that crack or fill with roots. Repeated slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line, not the sink. A camera look through sewer inspection shows the cause before anyone digs.
